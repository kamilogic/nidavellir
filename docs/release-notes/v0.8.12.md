Nidavellir 0.8.12 makes the Forge give more consistent profiles from one run to the next.

- Changed: the Forge now pins down each level's lowest stable voltage to a single step, by also testing the step it used to skip. A crash there is expected: the run recovers and continues on its own, up to 12 times per run.
- Changed: profiles keep a slightly larger margin for games, about 43 mV above the lowest voltage that passed. On the test RTX 3060 Ti, the same measurements now give Brokkr's Best 1800 MHz at 875 mV instead of 868 mV, which was unstable in games.
- Changed: clocks move in the 15 MHz steps every NVIDIA card uses, and voltages in millivolts, so RTX 40 and 50 cards follow the same rules as RTX 30 cards.
- Fixed: near the card's power limit, readings 1 W apart could send two runs down different paths, or pick the hotter of two equal top profiles. A clock that drops at the power limit now counts as the power limit, and equal profiles go to the lower voltage.
- New: if Windows is set not to restart after a blue screen, the Forge warns you when a run starts, because the run could not continue on its own.
- New: the menu bar stays at the top of the window while you scroll.

A run can take up to about an hour longer. Your forged profiles are kept. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

Nidavellir 0.5 forges three GPU profiles from your own card's measurements.

- Three profiles, measured on your GPU: Godforge (highest clock it sustains), Brokkr's Best (recommended: close to Godforge with less power) and Deep Calm (lowest power and heat).
- Every profile keeps a safety margin for games above the lowest voltage that passed the tests.
- A GPU driver crash during a Forge no longer loses the run. Turn on "Continue automatically after a driver crash" and it continues by itself after a driver reset or the next Windows start.
- Clearer screens: each profile shows its clock, voltage, power and the change versus stock. Technical details moved to "Run details" and Advanced diagnostics.
- Nidavellir now checks for updates when it starts and shows what changed before installing.
- NVIDIA GPUs only.

Tested on an RTX 3060 Ti. Try a profile in your own games before relying on it; Safe Loop returns the GPU to stock if something fails.

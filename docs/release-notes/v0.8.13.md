Nidavellir 0.8.13 fixes a Forge run on the RTX 5070 that lowered the voltage step after step until the driver crashed.

- Fixed: the RTX 5070 reports its voltage 5 mV under the one the Forge sets. The Forge took that for a voltage limit and kept lowering the voltage at a clock the card could not hold, until the driver crashed. It now reads that as the voltage it set, and when the card cannot hold a clock, the Forge moves to the clock the card did hold.
- Fixed: when a crash ended a Forge run, no error report was sent, even with automatic sending on. That report now goes out right away.

Your forged profiles are kept.

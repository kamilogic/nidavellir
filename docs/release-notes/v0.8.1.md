Nidavellir 0.8.1 keeps an overnight Forge from getting stuck after a GPU driver crash, and sends error reports even when the app is closed.

- If a graphics test stops responding after a GPU driver crash, the Forge no longer waits all night: it moves on within a minute and, with auto-continue on, restarts Windows and continues by itself.
- Error reports now go out from Nidavellir's background service, so they also arrive with the app closed, after Nidavellir restarted Windows, or while a Forge is stuck. They still need your OK in Settings → Error reports.
- New reports when the driver crashes during a Forge, when a Forge stops making progress, and when a blue screen or a power loss cuts a Forge off.

Your forged profiles are kept.

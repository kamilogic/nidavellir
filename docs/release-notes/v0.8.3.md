Nidavellir 0.8.3 sends more detailed error reports, so GPU problems on any card from the GTX 10 to the RTX 50 series can be found and fixed faster.

- When a graphics test crashes the GPU driver, the report now says which test step was running and, for DirectX 12, which command the GPU stopped at and why Windows reset it.
- Reports also describe the card and the PC around it: the exact chip and board, VBIOS, PCIe link, power limits, memory, display modes and which GPU tools are open (by name only). They still never hold your name, your PC's name or your files.
- Fixed: one of the Forge's memory tests could overflow the video memory of cards with 4 GB or less.

Your forged profiles are kept.

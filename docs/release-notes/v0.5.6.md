This update fixes the slowdown that built up over hours while Nidavellir was open.

- Nidavellir keeps one connection to NVIDIA's monitoring library instead of reopening it several times a second.
- Fixed: the NVIDIA driver slowly filled up with leftover data while Nidavellir ran, which made video stutter after many hours and could freeze the PC for a long time after Exit.

If your PC felt slow before this update, restart it once to clear what was left behind.

Nidavellir 0.7.1 lets an overnight Forge carry on by itself when a driver crash needs a Windows restart.

- When a driver crash needs a fresh Windows start, Nidavellir now shows a 2-minute warning, then restarts Windows by itself, and the Forge continues after the restart. Cancel the warning to keep working; the run then waits for you.
- Save your work in other programs before you leave a Forge running: a restart closes them without saving.
- Fixed: closing Nidavellir during a Forge, or cancelling the automatic continuation countdown, no longer turns "Continue automatically" off for every later Forge.

Your forged profiles are kept.

Nidavellir 0.8.4 fixes a problem in 0.8.3: if the GPU driver crashed during a DirectX 12 test, the Nidavellir service could stop as well.

- Fixed: the service now stays up after a driver crash in a DirectX 12 test, so the Forge recovers as usual and the error report is sent.
- Error reports say which command the GPU stopped at as soon as a DirectX 12 test crashes, and a report is sent even when a Forge run stops before its first result.
- Fixed: error reports showed only part of the VBIOS version.

Your forged profiles are kept.

Nidavellir 0.8.7 records more detail about how your graphics card behaves under load, so the Forge can be tuned for cards like the RTX 50 series.

- Error reports and the Forge log now show what the card itself reports during each test: the reasons it limited its clock, the voltage it ran at, and the clock its own curve gives at that voltage.
- Nothing changes in how the Forge tests, chooses or applies settings.

Your forged profiles are kept.

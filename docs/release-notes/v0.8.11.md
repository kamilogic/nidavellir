Nidavellir 0.8.11 helps the Forge find profiles on RTX 50-series cards, whose factory curve lowers its clocks as the card warms up.

- Fixed: when the card stays one voltage step below the one the Forge sets, the Forge now really tries the same clock at the voltage the card runs. Version 0.8.9 described this, but it never ran.
- Fixed: as the card warms up, its own factory curve lowers the clock the Forge raised (about 30 MHz on an RTX 5070). The Forge counted that as the card failing to hold its clock. It now checks the clock against the one the card itself set at that temperature, and profiles show the clock the card really runs when warm.

Your forged profiles are kept.

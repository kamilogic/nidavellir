Nidavellir 0.8.19 lets the Forge tune RTX 50 graphics cards.

- Fixed: an RTX 50 card lowers its own clock at a given voltage as it works, by up to about 60 MHz, so the Forge read every test as a dropped clock and finished without profiles. On RTX 50 cards the Forge now follows the clock the card actually runs, and an applied profile never goes above the highest clock the Forge tested it at. RTX 30 and 40 cards work exactly as before.

Your forged profiles are kept. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

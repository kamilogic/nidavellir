Nidavellir 0.8.21 is a new fix for RTX 50 graphics cards, where the Forge still stopped after a few minutes without profiles. RTX 30 and 40 cards work exactly as before.

- Changed: an RTX 50 card runs slightly under the voltage point it is locked to, on the way down to the next point. That point stayed at its stock clock, so the card lost up to about 40 MHz. The Forge now raises it too, so the card runs close to the clock being tested. No point is set above that clock.
- Changed: RTX 50 cards do not report when their power limit holds them back, so heavy tests read that as a dropped clock. The Forge now tells from the power the card draws.
- Changed: on RTX 50 cards the Forge no longer tests at 875 mV or below, where the driver is reported not to hold the voltage.

These changes have not run on an RTX 50 card yet. A Forge there can now take up to about 9 hours and may crash the driver at the card's limit, as on any card, and the Forge recovers from that. Your report helps if you allow it.

Your forged profiles are kept. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

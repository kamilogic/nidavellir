Nidavellir no longer causes small stutters while you play.

- The Sentinel's GPU check while gaming is now off by default. Turn it on in Settings when you want an analysis.
- Nidavellir asks you to turn the check on when the Sentinel records a problem or when the applied profile has little evidence behind it.
- Driver-crash detection stays on all the time and costs nothing.
- Fixed: a short GPU self-test every 20 seconds under load caused brief stutters in games.

Nidavellir 0.8.10 fixes a Forge stop on cards that reach the top of their factory curve without hitting their power limit, such as the RTX 40 series.

- Fixed: after finding the card's limit, the Forge could plan a profile above the card's factory curve, with more voltage than the card already uses for that clock. The Forge refused it safely, but the run then ended without profiles. Every profile it plans now stays below the factory curve.
- When a test is stopped because the card did not hold its clock, the Forge log now shows which part of the test lost the clock, and at what temperature.

Your forged profiles are kept. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

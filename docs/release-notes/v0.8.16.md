Nidavellir 0.8.16 lets the Forge continue on its own after a blue screen.

- Fixed: after a test at the edge caused a blue screen and Windows restarted by itself, the automatic continuation was refused until someone opened Nidavellir. The run now continues on its own after the restart, without anyone signing in.

Your forged profiles are kept. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

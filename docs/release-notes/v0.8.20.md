Nidavellir 0.8.20 makes the Forge's results steadier from run to run and shows what undervolting does on your card.

- Changed: Brokkr's Best and Deep Calm now sit at 95% and 90% of your card's stock boost clock instead of following Godforge. On a card held back by its power limit, Godforge's clock can move a step with the room temperature; the other two profiles no longer move with it.
- Changed: after a test at the edge crashes, the Forge avoids the voltages likely to crash at lower clocks too. It used to assume a fixed rate, which kept Deep Calm a step or two above its real limit. It now uses the rate your card's own failures show, never faster, and the fixed rate beyond the clocks those failures cover.
- New: the Stock column says whether your card is held back by its power limit under heavy load. That tells you whether undervolting gives back clock or mainly saves power and heat. Profiles show the clock they actually hold under heavy load.

Your forged profiles are kept. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

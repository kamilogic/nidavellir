Nidavellir 0.8.23 keeps a Forge going when Windows restarts in the middle of it, for example for an update.

- Fixed: when Windows restarted during a Forge, Nidavellir did not get enough time to stop cleanly, so the restart counted as a driver crash at the setting being tested. The Forge then avoided that setting and the ones near it for the rest of the run, which could leave the profiles a little more cautious than your card allows. Windows now waits for Nidavellir to stop, and a restart no longer counts as a crash.
- Changed: a Forge that Windows pauses by restarting now continues by itself after the restart, when automatic continuation is on and Windows is back within 30 minutes. After a longer break it waits for you, and if you pressed Stop first, your Stop stays. The option is now called "Continue automatically after a driver crash or Windows restart".
- Changed: error reports now say who restarted Windows and whether it shut down cleanly. They never include your computer name, your account or a restart comment.

Your forged profiles are kept. If a Windows restart was counted as a crash before this update, that record stays; Full Reset clears it, together with your profiles. A Forge run that was paused before this update cannot be resumed; start a new one.

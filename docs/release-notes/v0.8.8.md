Nidavellir 0.8.8 fixes a Forge stop on RTX 50-series cards and records more detail about how the card holds its clock.

- Fixed: after the first test, the Forge could pick a setting that was not a real undervolt for that voltage. It then stopped with "could not be tested safely". It now only picks valid settings.
- Error reports and the Forge log now show, three times during each test, the clock the card's own curve gives at the voltage it runs, and how often each clock and voltage was seen.
- Nothing else changes in how the Forge tests or applies settings.

Your forged profiles are kept.

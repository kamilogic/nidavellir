Nidavellir 0.7 brings a redesigned screen, a Live log in plain English and a Forge that carries on by itself after a driver crash.

- A new look: profiles, the last Forge and the live GPU readings sit in clean sections, and the profile in use glows like heated metal.
- The menu now has four pages: Forge, Live log, Sentinel and Settings.
- Live log follows the Forge step by step in English: green passed, white is a routine step, yellow needs attention and red failed.
- Live GPU shows the last minute of clock, voltage, power and temperature against your profile. Settings can switch it to gauges.
- Sentinel has its own page: what it protects, what happened and the points blocked on your GPU.
- Before a Forge starts, a short sheet explains what to expect.
- After a driver crash, the Forge now continues by itself: Nidavellir waits 2 minutes, which you can cancel, then picks up the same run. If you had turned this off, it stays off.
- Start over moved to Settings, and Full Reset asks you to type ERASE.
- Game Trace and Manual point are gone from the app: they were tools for development.

Your forged profiles are kept.
